Your Mac is not run by one all-powerful piece of software. It is run by a dysfunctional bureaucracy.
On Your Processor is a field guide to the overlapping jurisdictions inside modern macOS and Apple Silicon: Boot ROM and iBoot deciding what may run, XNU governing the kernel, launchd raising userspace, WindowServer owning the graphical world, the MMU and DART enforcing memory borders, the Secure Enclave guarding a security domain of its own, and a cast of daemons with enough private entitlements to make any organizational chart cry.
Through technical explanation, reverse-engineering receipts, and increasingly hostile family dialogue, the book asks one question over and over: who is actually in charge?
The answer is always the same.
It depends what you are trying to do.
Because inside a modern computer, authority has a jurisdiction.
