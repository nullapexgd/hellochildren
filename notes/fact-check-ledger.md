# Fact-Check Ledger

The ledger protects the prose from becoming more confident than its evidence. “Source conversation observation” refers to commands, strings, or entitlement dumps supplied in the referenced conversation; those observations should be reproduced on the target macOS build before publication.

| Claim | Status | Chapter | Basis | Editorial constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apple silicon boot starts with Boot ROM and proceeds through verified boot stages. | Proven | 02 | [Apple Platform Security: Boot process for a Mac with Apple silicon](https://support.apple.com/guide/security/boot-process-secac71d5623/web) | Do not flatten current LLB, LocalPolicy, and iBoot behavior into a universal historical sequence. |
| Boot ROM is immutable code established during SoC fabrication and forms a hardware root of trust. | Proven | 02 | [Apple Platform Security: Hardware security overview](https://support.apple.com/guide/security/hardware-security-overview-secf020d1074/web) | “Cannot be uninstalled” is comic paraphrase, not a service guarantee. |
| XNU supplies process, virtual-memory, scheduling, IPC, filesystem, networking, and driver mechanisms. | Proven at architectural level | 03 | XNU source and standard Darwin architecture; verify release-specific details against [Apple OSS Distributions](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu) | Do not claim XNU alone implements every high-level policy. |
| `launchd` performs system initialization and manages on-demand daemons and agents. | Proven | 04 | [Apple Daemons and Services Programming Guide](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/MacOSX/Conceptual/BPSystemStartup/Chapters/CreatingLaunchdJobs.html) | Archived documentation describes older OS X releases; avoid claiming every modern internal detail. |
| `launchd` is PID 1 in the root system context. | Proven at platform level | 04 | Apple daemon lifecycle documentation and reproducible `ps` observation | Per-user launchd instances and bootstrap domains complicate the family metaphor. |
